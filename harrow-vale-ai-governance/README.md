# Harrow & Vale LLP — AI governance for a small professional-services firm

**Small-business AI governance, delivered the way an MSP's client would receive it.**

Meridian Sextant shows AI governance for an AI *provider*. This project shows the other end of the market: a 48-person accounting, tax and advisory firm that **uses** AI tools, and whose largest client, a regional bank, has sent a supplier security questionnaire with a new section of AI questions.

The pack is delivered white-label: it carries the managed service provider's brand, not the author's, because that is how the work reaches small firms.

## What is in the pack

1. **Engagement summary:** what discovery found (9 AI tools in use, leadership aware of 3; client data in personal AI accounts; an AI note-taker recording client calls without consent) and what changed in three weeks.
2. **AI system inventory (HV-AIG-INV-001):** every tool with owner, users, data class and an approve, restrict or ban decision. ChatGPT Business and Claude Team approved in company workspaces with single sign-on.
3. **AI acceptable use policy (HV-AIG-POL-001):** one page, signed by the managing partner.
4. **Approvals log (HV-AIG-APR-001):** the basis for each decision, including vendor terms and review dates.
5. **AI literacy training record (HV-AIG-TRN-001):** 48 of 48 staff trained, tested and acknowledged.
6. **Questionnaire responses (HV-AIG-QST-001):** 14 AI questions answered, each linked to evidence, approved by the client.
7. **Evidence index and framework mapping (HV-AIG-EVD-001):** mapped to ISO/IEC 42001 Annex A, NIST AI RMF, the FTC Safeguards Rule, IRC §7216, UK GDPR and the EU AI Act.

## How it differs from Meridian

| | Meridian Sextant | Harrow & Vale |
|---|---|---|
| Organisation | 320-person AI platform unit serving banks | 48-person accounting firm |
| AI role | Builds and provides AI systems | Uses third-party AI tools |
| Main driver | DORA, client contracts, EU AI Act | A customer's security questionnaire |
| Typical controls | Agent permissions, residency, oversight evidence | Tool approvals, data rules, training records |
