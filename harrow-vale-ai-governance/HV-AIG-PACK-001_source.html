1. Engagement summary

About the client

Harrow & Vale LLP is an accounting, tax and advisory firm with 48 staff: 42 in Columbus, Ohio, and 6 in Manchester, UK. It prepares tax returns, audits and advisory work for about 900 businesses and individuals. IT runs on Microsoft 365 Business Premium, managed by [MSP name]. Staff mostly use ChatGPT and Claude; Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat is available to everyone through their work sign-in.

Why this work happened

On 2 October 2026 the firm's largest client, a regional bank, sent its annual supplier security questionnaire. This year it contained a new section of 14 questions on AI: inventory, policy, approvals, data handling, training and third-party AI. The firm had no written answers and no evidence behind them.

3. AI acceptable use policy HV-AIG-POL-001 · v1.0 · effective 21 Oct 2026

Purpose. To let our people use AI to work better while protecting client data, meeting our legal duties and keeping professional judgment with a person.

Applies to all partners, staff, contractors and interns in the US and UK, on any device used for firm work.

1. Use only approved tools

The approved list is the AI inventory (HV-AIG-INV-001), kept on the intranet. If a tool is not on it, do not use it for firm work. This includes AI features newly switched on inside software we already use.

2. Match the data to the tool

DataExamplesAllowed in
PublicPublished tax guidance, our websiteAny approved tool; personal AI accounts
InternalTemplates, internal notes without client detailsAI-01, AI-02, AI-03, AI-04
ConfidentialClient financial and tax data, personal data, HR, engagement lettersAI-01 and AI-02 (company workspaces only, signed in with your work account), and AI-05 in its approved mode

Never enter passwords, bank or card numbers, or Social Security / National Insurance numbers into any AI tool.

3. A person stays responsible

You own anything you send, file or sign, whoever drafted it. Check AI output against source documents. AI must not make final decisions on tax positions, audit opinions, hiring or client acceptance.

4. Be open about AI

Ask for consent before any AI transcription of a client call. Our website chatbot must say it is AI. Tell your manager if AI produced a material part of a client deliverable.

5. New tools and features

Request through the IT helpdesk form "AI tool request". The AI governance lead decides within 5 working days, using the approval checklist (data use, training on our data, retention, location, security terms, US and UK privacy terms). Decisions are logged in HV-AIG-APR-001.

6. Training

Everyone completes AI literacy training on joining and once a year, and acknowledges this policy.

7. Report problems

If client data went into an unapproved tool, or AI output caused an error, tell the AI governance lead the same day. We handle it as a security incident under the incident response plan; no blame for reporting promptly.

8. Breaches and review

Breaches are handled under the staff handbook. The policy is reviewed every 12 months, or sooner if the law, our tools or our clients' requirements change.
